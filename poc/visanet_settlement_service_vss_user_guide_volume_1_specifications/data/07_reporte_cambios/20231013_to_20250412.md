- Párrafo cambiado

The optional issuer fee is also listed in the clearing currency.'
  - Ahora: 'The Currency Conversion Fees Report (VSS–210) and Monthly Currency Conversion Fees Report (VSS–210–M) list currency-conversion information for issuers.
